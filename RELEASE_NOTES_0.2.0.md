# Release Notes — v0.2.0

## What's New

1. **💾 Save/Load System** — Pick up your adventure anytime with persistent game state
2. **🎬 6 Unique Endings** — Master Keeper, Legend, Explorer, Gentle One, Dropout, and Default paths
3. **🏆 Achievement System** — 8 unlockable badges including First Catch, Bonded, Master Collector, and Exhibition Champion
4. **🎒 Inventory System** — Healing herbs, capture charms, companion treats, and explorer maps
5. **⚔️ Dynamic Encounters** — Random events: storms, healing springs, lost travelers, wild sightings
6. **📊 Skill System** — Four skills (Strength, Agility, Smarts, Spirit) with primary/supplementary focus
7. **🧙 NPC Shopkeepers** — Buy, sell, and trade with Ridgecamp vendors
8. **🪙 Coin Economy** — Earn coins from captures and exhibitions
9. **🎭 Exhibition Mode** — Compete with companions for glory, crowd reactions, and perfect scores
10. **🦎 16 Creatures** — Expanded from 6 to 16 unique catchable creatures across 6 regions

## Stats

## How to Play
Train your skills in Act 1 (ages 7-10), then explore regions to find and catch creatures. Build bonds, manage inventory, and compete in exhibitions. Each playthrough can unlock different endings based on your choices — catch them all, explore everything, or take the gentle path. Save anytime and continue your adventure later!

```bash
python adventure_game.py
```
