🌲 Wildlands: Orb Catcher 🌟

Train your skills. Catch creatures. Build bonds. Win glory.

✨ v0.2.0 Features

  • 🎯 6 Unique Endings — Master Keeper, Legend, Explorer, Gentle One, Dropout, and more
  • 💾 Save/Load System — Pick up your adventure anytime
  • 🏆 Achievement System — 8 badges to unlock including First Catch, Bonded, and Master Collector
  • 🎒 Inventory System — Healing herbs, capture charms, companion treats, and explorer maps
  • ⚔️ Dynamic Encounters — Storms, healing springs, lost travelers, wild sightings
  • 📊 Skill System — Strength, Agility, Smarts, Spirit with primary/supplementary focus
  • 🧙 NPC Shopkeepers — Buy, sell, and trade with Ridgecamp vendors
  • 🪙 Coin Economy — Earn coins from captures and exhibitions
  • 🎭 Exhibition Mode — Compete with companions for glory and perfect scores
  • 🗺️ 5 Distinct Regions — Sunmeadow, Old Ruins, Silver River, Storm Canyon, Moonwood

🎮 How to Play

  1. Train (Ages 7-10): Start in Act 1 — train your four skills by answering Keeper questions and choose your primary focus.
  2. Explore: Visit regions to find creatures. Each has unique behaviors — some are timid, some brave, some playful.
  3. Capture: Use the right orb type, time your approach, and build bonds with your companions.
  4. Compete: Enter exhibitions to showcase your companions. Match strategy to personality for high scores!
